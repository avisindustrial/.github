# Code of conduct

Everyone who works in `avisindustrial` repositories, including employees, contractors and vendors, is expected to keep our spaces professional, respectful and inclusive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in code reviews, issues and discussions. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give and accept feedback openly.
- Respect confidentiality. Do not share internal code, data or discussions outside the people who need them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or personal attacks of any kind
- Offensive or demeaning comments, images or language
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that violates Avis Industrial's employee policies or a contributor's contractual obligations

## Reporting

Report concerns to your manager, to Human Resources, or by email to **[security@avisindustrial.com](mailto:security@avisindustrial.com)**. Reports are handled promptly and as confidentially as possible. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Organization owners may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may restrict or remove access to the organization. Employees and contractors are also subject to Avis Industrial's policies and their agreements.
